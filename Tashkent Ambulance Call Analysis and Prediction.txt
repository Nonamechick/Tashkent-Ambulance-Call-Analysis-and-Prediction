2. Project Overview
* Project Title: Tashkent Ambulance Call Analysis and Prediction
* Selected Field: Option B — Data Analysis & Prediction (Polynomial Regression)


Dataset Details:
* Dataset Title: Monthly Ambulance Calls by Age Category and District in Tashkent City
* Data Source: Open Data Portal of Uzbekistan (data.gov.uz)
* Dataset Size: 510 rows × 17 columns
* Selection Reason: Fulfills the project requirement for localized Uzbekistan web data and supports multi-variable demographic analysis alongside non-linear regression modeling.


3. Objectives & Expected Outcomes
Objectives:
1. Demographic Breakdown: Identify which age demographics generate the highest volume of emergency medical requests.
2. Geographic Distribution: Pinpoint high-demand districts across Tashkent city for emergency dispatch optimization.
3. Trend Forecasting: Fit a 2nd-degree Polynomial Regression model to forecast monthly call trajectories and account for seasonal shifts.


Expected Outcomes:
1. Predictive Model: A working 2nd-degree Polynomial Regression model () trained to predict overall call volume in Tashkent.
2. Visual Analytics: Interactive line plots and scatter charts showing monthly call distributions and projected workload trends.
3. Resource Insights: Actionable data findings to help healthcare authorities allocate medical personnel and ambulance fleets effectively across Tashkent districts.
